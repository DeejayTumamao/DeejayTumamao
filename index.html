Process

The DT Systems Transformation Framework

A structured approach from understanding the current business to building, implementing, and continuously improving the future-state system.

Assess

Understand the business model, technology, processes, customer journey, data, and operational bottlenecks.

Diagnose

Identify inefficiencies, risks, duplication, manual work, technology gaps, and opportunities for improvement.

Design

Create the future-state operating model, system architecture, workflows, customer journeys, and roadmap.

Transform

Implement the required systems, automations, CRM improvements, integrations, and process changes.

Transition

Manage migration, adoption, documentation, training, risk, and change management.

Optimize

Measure performance, identify new opportunities, and continuously improve the operating system.

The goal isn't to automate everything. The goal is to build a system that makes the right work easier, clearer, and more scalable.
