Overview

The Business Context

The business operates across multiple brands covering coaching, education, publishing, events, books, newsletters, and digital learning. The planned transformation involves consolidating and integrating multiple customer journeys, technology platforms, payment systems, marketing workflows, and educational assets into a more unified operating ecosystem.

Diagnosis

Business Audit Findings

01 — Fragmented Ecosystem

Multiple brands, platforms, customer journeys, and operational processes require a clearly defined architecture.

02 — CRM Governance

Contacts, tags, pipelines, lifecycle stages, customer information, and brand relationships require standardized data governance.

03 — Platform Consolidation

GoHighLevel is intended to become a central operating platform, but migration must be based on business requirements rather than simply moving technology.

04 — Course Migration Risk

Moving from Thinkific to GoHighLevel affects content, students, access permissions, payments, automations, and customer experience.

05 — Change Management

The transformation changes how the business operates, not simply which software it uses.

06 — Growth Opportunity

Existing courses, books, summit interviews, newsletters, and educational content can be transformed into connected lead-generation and customer journeys.
